### UNDERSTANDING BLOOD TOXICITY (0 - 100%)

In Sector 7, standing in **Toxic Pools (`~`)** or receiving strikes from mutagenic creatures immediately accumulates bio-toxins in your bloodstream.

```
[TOXICITY THRESHOLDS]
  0% -  25%: NOMINAL (No adverse symptoms)
 26% -  50%: MILD (Blurred vision, minor stamina drain)
 51% -  85%: SEVERE (Continuous tissue damage)
 86% - 100%: LETHAL (Systemic organ failure / Game Over)
```

### MITIGATION PROCEDURES
- Always carry **Anti-Toxin Serums** (reduces toxicity by `-40%`).
- Deploy **Chemical Flares** to light up dark corridors and avoid hidden slime patches.
- Equip **Mk.IV Chem-Respirator** or **Hazmat Vests** to block incoming toxicity damage.

### BEHAVIORAL STATE ENGINE

All hostiles in Sector 7 operate under dynamic state machines:

1. **PATROL (`👁 PATROL`)**: The creature moves along designated waypoints. It will not attack unless you step within its detection radius (4 tiles) or enter its line of sight.
2. **AGGRESSIVE (`⚔ AGGRO`)**: Triggered upon taking damage or detecting the player. Pursues relentlessly and deals full attack damage.
3. **FLEEING (`💨 FLEE`)**: When an enemy's HP drops below its morale break threshold (typically **30% HP**), it will attempt to flee away from the player.

### V.A.T.S. TACTICAL TARGETING
Use the **V.A.T.S.** button in the directional d-pad to calculate hit chances and execute targeted strikes against hostiles in range!
